# Learning Content Review Guidelines

## Purpose

This document defines the review process for learning content added to or updated within the Salesforce Learning Platform.

The purpose of review is to maintain technical quality, consistency, clarity, and alignment with the platform's learning structure.

## Review Areas

Content should be reviewed across the following areas.

### Scope and Learning Objective

Confirm that:

- The purpose of the topic is clear.
- The content remains focused on its intended subject.
- The level of detail is appropriate for the expected learner.
- Prerequisites are identified or reasonably covered.
- The material does not introduce unrelated concepts without context.

### Technical Accuracy

Review technical statements, examples, terminology, and implementation details for accuracy.

Where content depends on a specific technology, framework, platform, or version, confirm that the information is valid for the intended context.

Content that is uncertain, outdated, or no longer applicable should be corrected, updated, or clearly identified as historical.

### Learning Progression

Confirm that the material follows a logical sequence.

Review whether:

- Foundational concepts appear before dependent concepts.
- New terminology is explained when introduced.
- Examples support the concepts being taught.
- Advanced material does not unnecessarily interrupt beginner-level learning.

### Structure and Consistency

Confirm that:

- Files are placed in the appropriate learning area.
- Folder and file names follow repository conventions.
- The topic structure is consistent with related content.
- Navigation and relationships between topics remain clear.

### Clarity

Review the content from the learner's perspective.

Check for:

- Ambiguous explanations.
- Unexplained terminology.
- Unnecessary repetition.
- Missing context between sections.
- Examples that may be difficult to understand without additional explanation.

## References and Attribution

Where external sources, specifications, or official documentation are used to support the content, confirm that any required attribution or references are included.

External material should be used for research and verification rather than copied directly without permission or appropriate attribution.

## Review Outcome

After review, content may be:

- **Approved** — Ready to be included in the learning platform.
- **Approved with changes** — Minor corrections or improvements are required.
- **Needs revision** — Significant issues must be addressed before approval.
- **Outdated** — The content requires an update before it can remain current.

## Final Review Checklist

Before considering content complete, confirm:

- [ ] The learning objective is clear.
- [ ] The content is technically accurate.
- [ ] The learning progression is logical.
- [ ] Terminology is clear and consistent.
- [ ] Examples support the topic.
- [ ] Files and folders follow repository conventions.
- [ ] References or attribution are included where required.
- [ ] No unrelated or unnecessary content has been introduced.
