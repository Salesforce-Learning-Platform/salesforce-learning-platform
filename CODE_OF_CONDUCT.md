# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the Salesforce Learning Platform community.

We expect all contributors, learners, maintainers, and community members to interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful of different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing discussions on improving the learning platform and its content.
- Helping create a collaborative and inclusive learning environment.
- Respecting the time and effort of contributors and maintainers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Abusive, threatening, or offensive language.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or project activities.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and community interactions.
- Any other official communication channels associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Reports of unacceptable behavior should be submitted privately to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed appropriately and handled based on the circumstances.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by established open-source community standards and adapted for the Salesforce Learning Platform.
